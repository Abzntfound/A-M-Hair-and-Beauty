Hair care made clear.

Good hair care is not about using the most products. It is about understanding your routine, choosing products for the job you need them to do, and giving your hair consistent care. These guides explain simple habits you can use alongside the products in our collection.

Guide 01

Build a simple wash-day routine

A useful routine can be short. Start by cleansing the scalp and hair according to how much oil, sweat and product build-up you have. Follow with conditioner where your hair needs softness and easier detangling. After washing, handle wet hair gently and use a product that suits the finish you want.

  • Cleanse thoroughly without aggressively scrubbing the lengths.
  • Condition the lengths and ends, especially when they feel dry or tangled.
  • Detangle gradually, starting at the ends and working upwards.
  • Let your hair dry in a way that fits your hair type and styling routine.
Guide 02

Look after your scalp

Your scalp is part of your hair-care routine. Keep it clean, pay attention to how it feels, and avoid piling up products simply because they are marketed as stronger. If a product causes persistent irritation or an unusual reaction, stop using it and seek appropriate professional advice.

  • Keep your cleansing routine consistent.
  • Use only the amount of styling or oil product you need.
  • Wash build-up away rather than repeatedly adding more product.
  • Introduce new products one at a time so you can identify what works for you.
Guide 03

Protect hair while you sleep

Night-time care is mostly about reducing unnecessary friction and keeping your routine consistent. A satin bonnet can be one option for people who prefer covered hair while sleeping. Keeping the bonnet clean and choosing a comfortable fit are just as important as owning one.

  • Choose a bonnet or sleep method that stays comfortable overnight.
  • Clean your bonnet regularly according to its care instructions.
  • Avoid tying hair so tightly that it becomes uncomfortable.
  • Refresh your hair in the morning instead of repeatedly applying heavy product.
Guide 04

Choose products by purpose

Start with the result you actually need rather than buying several products with overlapping jobs. A shampoo is primarily for cleansing, conditioner is for conditioning and detangling, styling products help with hold or definition, and accessories can support how you maintain a style.

  • For cleansing: look at shampoos and how often you normally wash.
  • For moisture and detangling: consider a conditioner or treatment.
  • For styling: choose hold and finish based on the style you want.
  • For night protection: consider a bonnet or another low-friction option.
A practical routine

Four steps you can actually keep up with

Consistency matters more than having a complicated shelf. Adjust the frequency and products to your own hair, scalp and lifestyle.

1. Cleanse

Remove sweat, dirt and product build-up when your routine calls for it.

2. Condition

Use conditioner to make the hair easier to manage and style.

3. Style

Choose only the styling products needed for your intended look.

4. Maintain

Protect the style between wash days and keep accessories clean.

Hair-care questions

How often should I wash my hair?

There is no single schedule that suits everyone. Consider your scalp, activity level, styling routine and how quickly your hair becomes oily or has product build-up. The aim is a routine that keeps your scalp and hair comfortable and clean without making wash day unnecessarily difficult.

Should I use every product in a routine?

No. Start with the basics that solve your actual needs. Adding several products at once can make it harder to tell which product is useful and can create unnecessary build-up.

How should I introduce a new product?

Read the product instructions, use a small amount as directed and introduce one new product at a time. If you experience persistent irritation or another concerning reaction, stop using the product and seek appropriate professional advice.
